# Fraud detection (M8.2)

Factory streams (seeds 7, 11, 23; ~4957 invoices each, 100 cases per typology in test, 40 in cal, none in train). Isolation forests (amount group and timing/repeat group, 9 features) are fitted on clean train months after a history warm-up. An invoice alerts when any rule flag scores ≥ 0.5 or the forest score passes a threshold that spends the rest of a 1% false-positive budget after rule false positives. Cases with several invoices (splits) count as caught when any invoice alerts. Generated by `python -m evals.fraud`.

Gate: recall at 1% FPR on the test ROC (threshold set on the test clean invoices), minimum over seeds. Deployed: threshold chosen on clean cal invoices and applied unchanged to test.
